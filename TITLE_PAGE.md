### Data availability

The data and code that support the findings of this study are openly available at [https://github.com/sakshimaske303-commits/GHOST_INFRASTRUCTURE](https://github.com/sakshimaske303-commits/GHOST_INFRASTRUCTURE), with a permanently archived, version-controlled snapshot available via Zenodo at [https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21761320](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21761320). This includes the historical GIS datasets (digitized coal mine and worker-colony locations for Bochum and Essen), the network accessibility and statistical analysis scripts, and all output data (JSON results files) referenced in this manuscript.
